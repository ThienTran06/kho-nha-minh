KHO NHA MINH v1.7.0 - VAN HANH LAU DAI

1. Kiem ke thuc te
- Chon mat hang, nhap so dem thuc te, ngay gio va ghi chu.
- Tu tinh chenh lech voi ton he thong.
- Tu tao giao dich dieu chinh va cap nhat ton.
- Luu lich su 100 lan kiem ke gan nhat.

2. Danh sach can mua
- Tu dong lay cac mat hang cham muc ton toi thieu.
- Them truong "Muc muon co sau khi mua" cho tung mat hang.
- Tu goi y so luong can mua.
- Cho chon tung mon va xuat Excel.

3. Sao luu va khoi phuc
- Nut tai backup JSON trong Cai dat.
- Backup gom hang hoa, lich su nhap/xuat va chi phi.
- Co chuc nang khoi phuc co canh bao xac nhan.
- Co che backup database tu dong khi mo app van duoc giu nguyen.

4. Nhat ky thay doi
- Ghi lai them/sua/xoa hang, nhap/xuat, sua/xoa phieu, chi phi, kiem ke va khoi phuc backup.
- Xem 200 thao tac gan nhat tu Cai dat.

5. Chong tao trung
- Khi them thu cong, neu trung ten + don vi tinh voi hang da co, phan mem chan va yeu cau tim lai hang cu.

6. Giu nguyen cac phan da on dinh cua 1.6.2/1.6.3
- Xoa phieu cap nhat ton dung va dong bo giao dien ngay.
- Chon ngay gio phieu.
- Import XLS/XLSX.
- Bao cao Excel ngay/tuan/thang.
- Phieu nhap/xuat theo ngay.
- Lich su rieng tung mat hang.

7. Quen mat khau
- Lan dau tao mat khau, app sinh ma khoi phuc KHO-XXXX-XXXX.
- Co nut Quen mat khau o man hinh dang nhap.
- Nguoi dung cu nang cap len 1.7.0 co the vao Cai dat -> Tao ma khoi phuc.
- Tao ma moi se lam ma cu het hieu luc.
