MAVEN WRAPPER DA DUOC THEM VAO PROJECT

Windows:
  .\mvnw.cmd clean package -DskipTests

macOS/Linux:
  ./mvnw clean package -DskipTests

Lan chay dau tien wrapper se tu tai Maven 3.9.16 va kiem tra SHA-512.
Maven duoc luu trong thu muc .m2 cua nguoi dung, khong can cai Maven vao PATH.
Script installer/build-windows.ps1 cung da duoc doi sang dung mvnw.cmd.

May build Windows van can:
- JDK 21
- WiX Toolset 3.x (de jpackage tao installer EXE)
